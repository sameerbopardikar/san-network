# Aneek Combat Training OS

## Purpose

Make Aneek a good fighter by continuously turning the biggest current fighter-development gap into useful training and then updating the plan from evidence.

This borrows the useful idea from Personal OS and Founder OS, one durable goal decomposed into measurable components with a closed execution-learning loop, without copying their full governance machinery.

## Simple hierarchy

### 1. Goal

[[goals/aneek-fighter-development]]: **Make Aneek a good fighter.**

### 2. Components

- [[goals/aneek-functional-combat-coverage]]: technical skill across fighting ranges
- [[goals/aneek-pressure-tested-performance]]: fighting application and judgment under resistance
- [[goals/aneek-combat-capacity-and-durability]]: physical capacity for fighting
- [[goals/aneek-fighter-autonomy-and-reciprocal-peer]]: independent fighter development

These components define “good fighter.” They can be broken into skills and measures as the system learns.

### 3. Current State of Fighter

[[plans/aneek-state-of-fighter]] records what Aneek has learned, what is merely introduced, what survives resistance, current constraints, and the highest-value gap.

### 4. Training plan

[[plans/aneek-boxing-mma-curriculum]] is the cumulative plan. [[plans/aneek-boxing-mma-curriculum-12-week-block]] is the current block. They organize training but do not override current evidence.

### 5. Session

The next Calendar session targets the highest-value current gap. It contains the prescription, test, intensity boundary, and what must be recorded afterward.

### 6. Evidence and update

After training, collect Sameer's observations, Aneek's report, Arvy's analysis, observed/video evidence, and any credible outside validation separately. Update State of Fighter and future sessions only where the evidence warrants it.

## Controller loop

1. Read the goal and four components.
2. Read the latest State of Fighter and constraints.
3. Identify the component and subskill currently limiting progress most.
4. Choose the most useful next session, not blindly the next syllabus item.
5. Put the session on Calendar and execute it.
6. Record what held up, what failed, and under what conditions.
7. Update State of Fighter.
8. Revise future training and repeat.

## Control model

Sameer controls the software, curriculum, session plan, and adaptation logic. Inputs come from Sameer, Aneek, Arvy, actual performance, video where useful, and credible outside sources. Aneek can always choose whether and how he participates, but this does not require a constitutional or permission bureaucracy inside the software.

## Current application

The current limiting component is [[goals/aneek-pressure-tested-performance]]. Defensive hands, immediate counters, exits, and pivots have been introduced but are not proven under unpredictable resistance. The next session therefore tests and develops defense-counter-exit behavior under bounded live choice.
