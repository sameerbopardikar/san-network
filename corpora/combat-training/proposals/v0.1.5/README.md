# Aneek Combat-Training Shared Pack

Version: 0.1.5

Status: bilateral simplified Combat Training OS correction candidate

This package gives Sameer, Aneek, Expert, and Arvy one scoped combat-training knowledge plane without pooling either owner's private memory.

## Contents

- `architecture/combat-training-os.md`: simple goal → fighter components → current state → training plan/session → evidence/update loop, with Sameer controlling the software and planning.
- `knowledge/epistemic-contract.md`: claim classes, authority, conflict resolution, and technique-state vocabulary.
- `research/ufc-pi-integrated-programming.md`: first shareable research synthesis and exact source pointers.
- `research/combat-video-model-technique-analysis.md`: evidence-weighted review of current video-model capability, limitations, pilot design, and go/no-go thresholds for sparring analysis.
- `state/aneek-state-of-fighter.json`: canonical, versioned fighter state including skill maturity, explicit non-skills, session history, performance frontier, next-session inheritance, and changelog.
- `sessions/2026-08-01-week-01-session-a.md`: the original Session A coaching plan retained as historical evidence.
- `packets/arvy-adoption-contract.md`: destination-local ingestion, teach-back, prediction, and receipt contract.
- `manifest.json`: exact file hashes and release metadata.

## Roles

- Sameer supplies coach observations and controls live coaching execution.
- Aneek supplies embodied trainee evidence and controls his own participation, pain, consent, and private information.
- Expert integrates research, curriculum, and shared evidence.
- Arvy maintains Aneek-local context, teaches back shared knowledge, challenges Expert, makes testable predictions, and returns owner-authorized deltas.

## Privacy boundary

Included: boxing/MMA curriculum, training observations authorized by Sameer, public-source research synthesis, drill designs, technique-state data, and program decisions.

Excluded: raw private GBrains, unrelated health/personal/social/work context, credentials, secrets, private source bodies, and Aneek-private material that Aneek has not authorized Arvy to share.

## Adoption maturity

This repository artifact proves publication and transport. It does not prove Arvy indexed, retrieved, applied, or calibrated the material. Those states require Arvy's destination-local receipt and later outcome comparison.

- Adds the delegated research review across Gemini, GPT, Claude, Qwen, video benchmarks, sports AQA, pose estimation, and combat-specific multi-camera research.
