# Epistemic and Training-State Contract

## Claim classes

Every material training claim must retain one class:

- `coach_observation`: Sameer's direct report of what he coached or observed.
- `trainee_self_report`: Aneek's direct report of sensation, comprehension, confidence, pain, or reproducibility.
- `observed_performance`: video, scored drill, sparring behavior, timing, or another externally inspectable result.
- `external_source_claim`: what a research paper, manual, coach, or instructional source actually says.
- `expert_inference`: Expert's analysis or recommendation.
- `arvy_inference`: Arvy's analysis or recommendation.
- `adopted_program_decision`: the current curriculum choice, with reason and reversal condition.

Agreement between agents is not proof. Agent inference never silently becomes a human firsthand fact.

## Claim-specific authority

- Sameer controls what he coached and directly observed.
- Aneek controls his sensations, comprehension, pain, confidence, consent, and participation.
- Video, scores, and live outcomes control observable performance claims.
- Sources control what those sources say, not whether the claim applies to Aneek.
- Expert and Arvy may interpret, challenge, and recommend.

## Conflict resolution

- Preserve conflicts instead of overwriting one side.
- Resolve factual conflicts with the primary artifact or direct speaker.
- Resolve performance conflicts with a bounded drill, scored test, or video.
- Resolve source disagreements by preserving both claims, applicability, and evidence quality.
- Material changes to goals, contact intensity, sparring freedom, risk posture, spending, or overall curriculum direction must be visible to Sameer and Aneek before adoption.

## Technique-state vocabulary

Track each capability as one or more of:

- `not_introduced`
- `introduced`
- `clean_isolated_drilling`
- `successful_reaction_drilling`
- `successful_constrained_sparring`
- `retained_fatigue_contact`
- `externally_validated`
- `regressed_needs_repair`

Mechanics, recognition, timing, force control, and integration may have different states. A clean pad repetition is not live competence.

## Session writeback minimum

After each session record:

- date and duration;
- session/block/version;
- modalities actually completed;
- techniques trained;
- coach observations;
- trainee-authorized self-report;
- what held under resistance;
- what failed and at which progression;
- contact intensity;
- pain, soreness, or fatigue relevant to training;
- scored gate result;
- video/source pointers where available;
- one inherited next-session priority.
