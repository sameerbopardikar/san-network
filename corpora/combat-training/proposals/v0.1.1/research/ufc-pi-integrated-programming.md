## High-confidence operating principles

- Start from actual sport demands rather than generic conditioning. [UFC PI 2021, pp. 10, 13–29]
- Profile the whole fighter and identify the current limiting factors before prescribing. [pp. 42–139]
- Individualize programming around schedule, injuries, style, technical needs, and response. [pp. 10, 140–195]
- Monitor and revise using both objective and subjective evidence. [pp. 168–192]
- Technical practice, sparring, wrestling, lifting, and conditioning share one recovery budget. [pp. 152–177, 352–367]
- Simple subjective measures such as session RPE, soreness, wellness, conversation, body language, and warm-up behavior can be decision-useful. [pp. 168–177]
