# Aneek Combat Training OS

## What it is

A domain-specific instance of the same goal-controller architecture established for Percival Founder OS and Expert OS. It is not a static syllabus, Calendar template, video analyzer, or pile of training notes. It continuously converts the governing North Star into evidence-bearing training interventions.

## Hierarchy

### Tier 0 — Constitution

- Aneek owns his body, end state, commitments and risk tolerance.
- Sameer owns his coaching participation, boxing judgments and partner-safety boundaries.
- Skill claims must match evidence maturity.
- Resistance and complexity progress before impact.
- Broader MMA claims require credible references and eventual external validation.

### Tier 6 — Runs

A coached session, solo assignment, open mat, external assessment or video-analysis execution is a run. Google Calendar is the execution surface. A completed run may produce evidence but cannot by itself advance the mission or goal.

## Persistent controller loop

1. Re-read North Star and active strategic goals.
2. Compile current State of Fighter, constraints, available partners/equipment, soreness and new directives.
3. Identify the controlling gap against the hierarchy.
4. Select one bounded mission/probe and define the prediction, prescription, evidence and gate.
5. Put the executable run on Calendar while preserving parent ancestry.
6. Execute and collect source-separated evidence.
7. Reconcile outcome against the gate; preserve uncertainty and contradictions.
8. Update State of Fighter and relevant strategic-goal state.
9. Continue, regress, pivot, pause or close the probe/mission.
10. Revise future Calendar runs and curriculum only where evidence warrants it.

## Controller selection rule

The highest-value next session is not automatically the next syllabus page. Rank candidate interventions by North-Star contribution, current weakness, information value, safety, transfer, available conditions and whether the result can be measured. Prefer the smallest intervention that forces reality to answer the controlling uncertainty.

## Goal altitude versus execution type

North Star and strategic goals describe desired reality. Program, mission, probe and run describe how the OS changes reality. State of Fighter describes current evidence. Calendar displays prescriptions and executions. These objects must not be collapsed.

## Autonomy overlay

Expert and Arvy may autonomously research, draft sessions, update evidence projections, propose/reorder probes and adapt future reversible training prescriptions inside the approved mission. They may not silently change the North Star, impose new competition commitments, increase consequential contact/risk, spend money, or overrule Aneek/Sameer.
